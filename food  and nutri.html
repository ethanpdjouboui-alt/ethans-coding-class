A healthy meal should consist of:

    1. A balance diet
    2. should not be too salty,sweet,oily/fatty
    3. should be attractive
    4. Should consists of protein,carbohydrates,mineral,vitamins,fats

A healthy plate is a meal that contains a balanced variety of foods, including vegetables and fruits, grains or other starchy foods, and protein, to provide the nutrients the body needs for growth, energy, and good health.

Example of a healthy meal

Ingredients

    6 frozen breaded chicken tenders (about 12 ounces)
    1 (10-ounce) package frozen cubed sweet potatoes
    ~10 cups mixed spring salad greens
    2 tablespoons lemon juice
    1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
    ½ teaspoon ground pepper, plus more for garnish
    2 small Honeycrisp apples, unpeeled and cut into ½-inch cubes
    ¼ cup crumbled blue cheese, divided
    ½ cup yogurt ranch dressing, divided

Nutrition Tips

Drink Enough Water

Water helps the body stay active, supports digestion, and keeps the brain focused. Students should carry a water bottle and drink water throughout the day.

Eat Colourful Food

Different colours in fruits and vegetables give different nutrients. A colourful plate often means a healthier plate.
