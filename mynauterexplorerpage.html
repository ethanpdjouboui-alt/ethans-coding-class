Learn about the different parts of nature

Forests

Forests are full of trees. They give us oxygen and are homes for many animals.

Rivers

Rivers carry fresh water from the mountains to the sea.

Mountains

Mountains are the highest places on Earth. Some have snow all year.

Wildlife

Wildlife means animals that live in nature, like birds, deer, and fish.

Protect Nature

We can protect nature by recycling, saving water, and planting trees.
